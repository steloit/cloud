## Findings

**1 · D6 cell economics conflate baseline with at-capacity.**
"One cell ≈ $2–3k/mo ≈ $25–50k MRR" cannot be the loaded cost: §6's own
marginal is $14.75/paying → $7.4–14.7k/mo at the 500–1,000 cap, and
50–60 workload nodes alone exceed $3k even on spot. $2–3k is the
baseline (Neon fleet, observability, floor, LBs). → A1.1

**2 · pgmq-on-substrate defeats D6's idle economics.**
Polled queues hold compute endpoints awake; scale-to-zero dies for
exactly the projects most likely to hold queues. LISTEN/NOTIFY and
naive shared pollers still require a held connection. → A1.2 (blocking
design constraint; NATS fallback accepted, losing branch-coherence)

**3 · N=1 Safekeeper vs D4's "unforgivable sin".**
Pre-payment databases ride one Safekeeper: its loss eats the WAL window
since last upload — unbounded, undisclosed loss under the document's
own standard. → A1.3 (bounded + published: RPO ≤ 5 min recommended,
WAL on PD, offload to GCS; pending ratification)

**4 · Presigned URLs leak the substrate (D4/D7 contradict D8).**
`storage.googleapis.com/<bucket>/…` is a substrate concept on a
customer-visible surface. → A1.4 (locked: Steloit-domain object URLs,
provider URLs never cross the boundary)

**5 · Three missing day-one shape invariants.**
(a) tenant-data restore drill (§2.3 rebuilds infra, not customer data);
(b) control-plane DB backup/DR (D9 makes it the source of truth, its
loss was uncovered); (c) security floor (workload identity, zero static
keys, signed images). → A1.5 (§2.9–2.11)

**6 · Starter topology contradiction.**
"Zonal, scale-to-zero nodes" cannot include the Neon fleet; and
duty-cycling the core pool is scheduled platform downtime, which should
be stated as a choice, not implied by a cost line. → A1.6

**7 · The region row hides a migration.**
us-central1 → Mumbai at design-partner demo migrates partner data —
which §3's own closing rule classifies as an incident. → A1.7 (modified:
partner-touchable environments born in asia-south1; founder dev may use
us-central1)

**8 · Two absent risks.**
GCP quota walls (fresh accounts don't get 50-node CPU quota; increases
gate growth) and free-compute abuse (scale-to-zero + free tier attracts
miners; gVisor is not an abuse control). → A1.8

**9 · Egress has transparency but no mitigation.**
§5 costs it ~$0.12/GB; §7's only answer is pricing. Standard-tier
networking and CDN-fronting unevaluated — and coupled to finding 4's
proxy decision. → A1.9

**10 · Housekeeping.**
D3 spike checklist unnamed (multipart, conditional writes / generation
fencing — Neon split-brain protection depends on it, list pagination);
"VictoriaMetrics/Mimir" is an unmade choice wearing a locked costume;
gVisor needs a grammar-named compatibility stance. → A1.10
