## Goal

The AI assistant conversation store: threads + messages persist independently
of the ai-assistant policy — `disabled` HIDES the surface (404 empty-equivalent
on create, omitted from list) but deletes nothing; re-enable restores verbatim
(AI Law 4).

## Read first

- openapi.yaml `/assistant/threads` (createThread 201 / 404-when-disabled;
  listThreads) + the Thread/Message/Evidence schemas.
- docs/product/13-ai/*.md — the four AI laws (Law 4: disable hides all surfaces,
  deletes nothing, instant re-enable).
- internal/identity/policy/policy.go — the ai-assistant policy kind (already
  narrows ai.* to 403 when disabled; this task adds the 404-invisible surface
  gate that Law 4 wants for /assistant/*).

## Approach

- Migration: `assistant_threads` (user+org owned, context jsonb, attached
  insight) + `assistant_messages` (role/content/evidence jsonb/proposal_id),
  both ON DELETE CASCADE from org/user/thread.
- `Service.AIAssistantEnabled(org)` — the ONE enablement source of truth (the
  T12.3 enforcement primitive): disabled is the only off state.
- Handlers gate on it FIRST (404 invisible), then authorize ai.use (no longer
  policy-narrowed once enabled) for membership + grant. listThreads unions the
  user's threads across their orgs, omitting disabled orgs (hidden-not-deleted).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] disable hides, never deletes; re-enable restores — proven end-to-end:
  create 2 threads → disable → create 404s, list hides both, DB still has 2 →
  re-enable → list restores both verbatim.
- [x] context.org required (422); non-member cannot create in another org.

## Out of scope

- postMessage (the AI turn — Law 2 evidence, Law 3 viewer-scoped retrieval)
  needs the model + retrieval plane (later E13 tasks); this is the STORE.
- T12.3 formalizes AIAssistantEnabled as the org-wide policy enforcement across
  ALL /assistant/* surfaces (insights, proposals) as they land.

## Related

AI Law 4 · T12.1 (ai-assistant policy vocab) · T12.3 (surface-wide enforcement)
· T13.x (postMessage, insights, proposals).

## Outcome

The thread store lands with the Law-4 gate as a first-class, tested guarantee:
`AIAssistantEnabled` is the single enablement predicate, checked BEFORE the
ai.use authorization so a disabled org returns 404 (the AI surface is
INVISIBLE, per Law 4) rather than the 403 the RBAC narrowing alone would give —
the surface must never admit it could exist. The disable path deletes nothing
(the integration test asserts the rows survive in `assistant_threads` while the
API hides them) and re-enable restores verbatim. This also delivers the T12.3
enforcement primitive: `AIAssistantEnabled` is the org-wide source of truth
every future /assistant/* surface (insights, proposals, postMessage) gates on.

Finding recorded: T12.3's declared `deps: [T12.1]` is incomplete — its
"gates ALL /assistant/*" AC depends on the assistant HTTP surface (this task and
later E13 endpoints) existing to gate; the enforcement primitive now exists, so
T12.3 becomes the wiring of AIAssistantEnabled across every assistant handler as
they land, plus the org-key/multi-org resolution contract. listThreads unions
across the user's orgs because the contract carries no org param — recorded so
a future ?org= filter (owner-level contract change) can scope it.
