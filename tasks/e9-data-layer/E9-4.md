## Goal

Queue capability = **pgmq inside the customer's Postgres**, consumed by a worker — not a separate service, broker, or product (ADR-0004/A5.2).

## Summary

The queue collapses to a Postgres capability: enable pgmq in the customer's CNPG database, document the worker-consumer pattern. Queue rows branch with the DB for free (they're *in* the DB); the consumer is a worker that scales to zero like any compute — no special queue infrastructure. **The A3.1 WAL-signal design review, the NATS fallback, and risk R3 are retired** (they existed only to build a separate branch-coherent queue service). Ships in V2 alongside workers (a queue without worker compute to drain it is half a product). Internal control-plane jobs remain on River (Architecture v1 §12) — same Postgres-native philosophy. Queue frames (S4/D8/D17/D18) re-home as a tab under the Postgres service, not a rail product.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] pgmq enabled per Postgres service on request; enqueue/consume/DLQ works from a worker
- [ ] Idle project consumes zero queue-specific compute (the queue is rows; the worker scales to zero)
- [ ] No separate queue service, broker, or NATS anywhere; surfaced as a Postgres tab
