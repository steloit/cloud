## Goal

Live tail (-f) over SSE

## Summary

**AC:** `steloit logs -f` streams; shared query language works verbatim (console log bar parity).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] `steloit events -f` live-tails the env spine over SSE (T2.5's stream): frames
      render as tail lines (or raw data under --json); heartbeats never surface.
- [x] Reconnects RESUME from the last frame's `id:` cursor — no gap, no duplicate —
      proven by a fake that closes the stream and asserts the reconnect's `?cursor=`.

## Outcome

- `tailEvents`: long-lived stream (no client timeout), cursor tracked per frame,
  1s backoff between reconnects, bounded or infinite reconnect budget; `readSSE`
  parses id/event/data/heartbeat framing with a 1MB line budget.
- `steloit logs -f` inherits this machinery verbatim when E6 lands the backend —
  the SSE contract is identical (x-streamable).
