## Goal

Quiet hours affect ROUTING only — never recording, never firing, and never the
security/paging class (glossary law; the notification-prefs contract states it).

## Read first

- internal/notify/notify.go Notify + quietNow — quietNow gates ONLY the email
  route; the bell (recording) and webhook are unaffected.
- openapi.yaml updateNotificationPrefs note: "Quiet hours affect ROUTING, never
  recording, and never gate escalation paging (glossary law)."

## Approach

The routing-only behavior existed (quietNow only skips email). US-10.2 turns the
law into an enforceable invariant and adds the escalation-paging escape hatch:
- NotifyInput.Urgent marks a security/paging-class notification; quiet hours
  NEVER gate it (the email routes even inside the quiet window).
- The invariant test pins: inside quiet hours a NORMAL notification is still
  RECORDED on the bell (durable) but its email is suppressed; an URGENT
  (security/paging) notification records AND routes its email; outside quiet
  hours a normal notification routes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Firing/recording during quiet hours: the bell record is created inside the
  quiet window (recording never gated); the alert-firing state is structurally
  unaffected (quietNow lives only in the email route, never in the state path).
- [x] Delivery deferred: a NORMAL notification's email is suppressed inside quiet
  hours (best-effort route, no deferral queue — the bell is the durable record).
- [x] Security/paging never gated: an Urgent notification's email routes inside
  quiet hours (proven with a pinned in-quiet clock — deterministic, no flake).

## Out of scope / findings

- The Urgent bypass is a tested PRIMITIVE; no security/paging notification SOURCE
  emits yet (the first — e.g. suspicious sign-in — arrives with the E7/auth
  security surface and will set Urgent:true). Recorded, not overclaimed.

## Related

Glossary law (quiet hours) · T10.3 (notify router) · #277 (notify wall-clock
flake — this test pins its clock for the same reason).

## Outcome

Quiet hours are now provably routing-only. The core law — recording and firing
are NEVER gated, only the best-effort email ROUTE is deferred during a member's
quiet window — is enforced by an invariant test with a pinned in-quiet clock
(deterministic, no wall-clock flake): a normal notification inside quiet hours is
recorded on the bell but its email is suppressed; outside quiet hours it routes.
The escalation-paging escape hatch (NotifyInput.Urgent) bypasses quiet hours
entirely and is tested (an Urgent notification's email routes even at 23:00 UTC).
Honest scope: no security/paging notification SOURCE sets Urgent yet — the
primitive is ready for the first one (E7/auth security surface); recorded, not
claimed as wired.
