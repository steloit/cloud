## Goal

An org's hard monthly spend bound is enforced by construction: at the cap, new provisioning pauses and soft-overage stops accruing; nothing is deleted; state kept; one click resumes on raise/renew.

## Summary

Flagship proof point #7 (messaging.md; F9 "the cap is real" clause, founder-approved 2026-07-18). Composes with the estimate gate: an estimate whose acceptance would cross the cap is **refused at accept time with the math shown**. This is the elevation of existing F9 machinery (budget endpoint B1, quota evaluator T11.5) to a named, demoable guarantee — the market has alerts-only, retrofits, and "coming soon"; we ship a bound.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] WHEN accepting an estimate would cross the org cap THEN accept is refused (402-family) with the math in remediation
- [ ] WHEN the cap is reached THEN provisioning pauses and soft-overage stops accruing; running services untouched; state kept
- [ ] Raising the cap or cycle renewal resumes instantly; every cap event on the events spine
- [ ] The cap is never breachable by any path (property test across provisioning + overage flows); safety features remain never-gated
