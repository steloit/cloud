## Goal

Soft quotas bill (confirm + price up front); hard quotas fail loudly; the 80%
threshold warns with the math (banner+bell+email). Plus the two coupling
invariants: safety is never plan-gated at the call site; soft overage halts at
the hard spend cap.

## Read first

- internal/quota/quota.go — Evaluate (soft/hard decision) + WarnLevel/ShouldWarn
  (the 80% threshold) — the T11.5 evaluator.
- internal/billing/plans.json — overage unit prices + IsNeverGated; the $note:
  per-plan EGRESS allowances are canon-DEFERRED (only Business 100 GB is pinned;
  Free/Pro unsourced — do not invent).
- docs/product/16-qa/qa.md scenario 2 — egress 87/100 GB → warn at 80% ~$1.62.

## Approach

The evaluator (soft/hard) + WarnLevel existed (T11.5); US-11.2 wires the warning
and closes the two coupling gaps as tested primitives:
- WarnMessage(meter, allowance, used, rate) → the 80% payload with the overage
  price; EmitQuotaWarning routes it to banner+bell+email via notify (proven
  end-to-end for the canon Business egress, QA scenario 2).
- GateCapability (billing): consults IsNeverGated BEFORE producing a plan gate —
  safety is never gated at the CALL SITE (closes the US-11.1 enforcement gap).
- ClampOverageToCap (quota): a running service's soft overage halts once the org
  reaches its hard monthly spend cap (closes the US-11.7 coupling gap).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] 80% warning: WarnMessage fires at >=80% of a finite allowance (never for
  unlimited) with the overage unit price; EmitQuotaWarning routes it to the bell
  (and email if on). Proven for QA scenario 2's canon egress (87/100, $0.09/GB).
- [x] Never-gated enforcement at the call site: GateCapability never gates a
  safety capability (table-tested for all seven), still gates a real one.
- [x] Soft-overage-halt-at-cap: ClampOverageToCap accrues only up to the cap and
  halts at/over it (table-tested incl. the boundary).

## Out of scope / findings

- The soft-quota GATE at action time (seats on invite) + hard 429+Retry-After
  (rate) already shipped (T11.5/invites/rate-limit); the spend-cap hard 402 is
  US-11.7. This task adds the WARNING + the two couplings.
- Per-plan meter allowances (egress/events/AI/builds for Free/Pro) are
  canon-DEFERRED (plans.json $note) — the warning mechanism is proven for the
  one canon-pinned allowance (Business egress); a rollup-time auto-trigger that
  warns every over-80% meter needs those allowances first. Recorded.
- The "~$1.62 at current pace" forecast + "no upsell when overage is cheaper"
  (B8) are console/forecast presentation concerns; the backend warning carries
  the concrete math (percent + unit price). Recorded.

## Outcome

The soft/hard quota machinery (T11.5) gains its 80% warning and its two missing
couplings, all as tested primitives. WarnMessage produces the QA-scenario-2
payload (fires at 80%, carries the overage unit price) and EmitQuotaWarning
routes it end-to-end to the bell/email (proven for the canon Business egress
87/100 at $0.09/GB). GateCapability closes the US-11.1 enforcement-path gap: the
capability gate consults IsNeverGated FIRST, so no safety capability is ever
plan-gated at the call site (table-tested for all seven). ClampOverageToCap
closes the US-11.7 coupling: a running service's soft overage accrues only up to
the hard spend cap and halts at/over it. Honestly deferred (recorded): per-plan
meter allowances for Free/Pro are canon-unsourced, so the rollup-time
auto-trigger over every meter awaits that data; the $1.62 forecast and
upsell-suppression are console/forecast concerns.
