## Goal

Invoices carry the same line grammar as estimates; every line sums to the
subtotal; money is integer cents end-to-end (ADR-025). Enforced by invariant
tests at the invoice (Go) and console layers — "what you were quoted == what
you're billed."

## Read first

- docs/plan/product-taxonomy-review.md §74 — the UNIFIED line rule: "every line
  on any Steloit surface is either a metered quantity at a published unit price,
  or a stated plan allowance; nothing else exists" (subsumes the B3 line→meter
  contract).
- internal/invoice/invoice.go Close — builds lines (plan fee + one per meter),
  Σ lines == total by construction; usage_ref is plan:* or meter:*.
- The B3 canon defect: apps/console/src/mocks/world.ts invoices[inv_2026_06] —
  lines (Σ 41492¢) do not sum to the printed total (47700¢); mock preserves it
  "as a finding, not silently fixed" pending the S5 ruling.

## Approach

The Go invoice layer already sums lines→total (T11.3). US-11.6 makes the grammar
enforced and mirrors it to the console:
- Go: strengthen the invoice test to the CLOSED §74 grammar — every line's
  usage_ref is plan:* OR meter:* (nothing else), integer cents.
- Console: a reusable `invoiceLinesSum` invariant with a PROVEN red state (a
  malformed / float-cents invoice fails), enforced over every mock invoice.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Shared rule enforced at the Go invoice layer AND the console layer: lines
  sum to the subtotal; integer cents; every line is plan-allowance or metered.
- [x] The console invariant has a proven red state (bad invoice fails), not an
  inert pass.

## Out of scope / findings (S5)

- The B3 numeric reconciliation (which value is authoritative — the itemization
  Σ 41492¢ or the printed total 47700¢, and the inconsistent tax) is an S5 canon
  ruling. This task does NOT invent the numbers: it enforces the §74 rule and
  DEFERS inv_2026_06 to S5, quantifying the exact gap in a test so the ruling has
  the evidence. When S5 rules, remove the B3 exception and the invariant covers
  it automatically.

## Related

ADR-025 (integer cents) · taxonomy §74 · T11.3 (invoice service) · S5 (B3 canon
ruling) · @steloit/canon assertArithmetic (the sibling project/org invariant).

## Outcome

The invoice arithmetic grammar is now an enforced invariant at both the Go
invoice layer (closed §74 grammar: every line plan:* or meter:*, integer cents,
Σ lines == total) and the console layer (a reusable invoiceLinesSum with a
proven red state — a mis-summing or float-cents invoice fails — enforced over
the mock invoices). The B3 canon defect (inv_2026_06 lines 41492¢ ≠ printed
47700¢) is NOT silently fixed: picking the authoritative number is an S5 canon
ruling, so the invariant defers that one invoice to S5 and a test quantifies the
exact gap as evidence for the ruling. Every other invoice — and every future
one, real or mock — is bound by the rule.
