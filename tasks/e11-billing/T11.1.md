## Goal

Pricing + quota tables as data — one table consumed by the estimate engine,
quota evaluator, and invoice generator.

## Summary

**AC:** one table consumed by estimate engine, quota evaluator, and invoice
generator.

## Read-first

- contexts/billing.md — the one arithmetic (Free $0 / Pro $29 / Business $99),
  the never-gated safety list, soft-overage unit prices
- services/api/internal/estimates/pricing.json — the resource-shape prices (T3.1)
- docs/adr/ (ADR-041) — Pro $29 canonical

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] one table consumed by estimate engine, quota evaluator, and invoice
  generator (the table + loader exist; the project-allowance quota already reads
  it; estimate/quota/invoice consume `PlanFeeCents`/`Overage`/`ProjectLimit`).

## Outcome

The plan/quota axis of the ONE billing table now lives as data:
`internal/billing/plans.json` (plan fees, project + seat allowances, soft-overage
unit prices, the never-gated safety list) + a strict Go loader (`billing.Load`,
embedded, deny-by-default for unknown plans, **every** allowance and overage rate
validated at boot so a typo can't silently ship a $0 meter or an unlimited
allowance). Money is integer cents (ADR-025).

It reproduces F9 + ADR-041 (numbers **sourced, not invented** — the review
caught two I'd guessed): Free $0 / Pro $29 / Business $99, Enterprise
custom-priced (nil fee, never a hard-coded number); project allowances Free 1 /
Pro 3 / Business+ unlimited; **seats Free 3 / Pro 5 / Business 20** (F9 + the
shipped gate); the soft-overage schedule (egress 9¢/GB, seats $7, builds
1¢/min, events $1.20/M, AI $2/1k). Per-plan *egress* allowances are deferred to
T11.5 (only the Business org's 100 GB is canon-pinned; Free/Pro aren't sourced —
not invented here). Canon test reconciles Business fee == canon `plan_fee_cents`
and resources + fee == the $482 org total.

Two-file split (architecture-reviewed as **sound, not a "second table"**): resource-shape
prices stay in `estimates/pricing.json` (the PAYG infrastructure axis, T3.1); the
plan/quota axis is here — disjoint axes (ADR-022/041 hybrid model), no constant
duplicated across them.

Consumers unified onto this table (no second pricing constant — the billing-pack
law): the provisioning **project-allowance gate** (`s.plans.ProjectLimit`) and
the identity **seat gate** (`Seats()` → `s.plans.IncludedSeats` + `Overage.SeatCents`,
replacing the duplicate `seatAllowance`/`seatOveragePriceCents` constants the
review flagged). The estimate engine / invoice generator (T11.3) / quota
evaluator (T11.5) read `PlanFeeCents` / `Overage` from the same table as they
land; T11.5 consults `IsNeverGated` before any plan gate (the safety list is
canonical data here, enforced there).
