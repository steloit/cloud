## Goal

QA scenarios 3 (dunning) and 4 (downgrade block) run in SECONDS under a
warped-clock harness — billing time never real-waits in a test.

## Approach

`warpedClock` (mutable now + Advance) injected through every billing clock seam
(`subscription.Service.WithClock` + a new `identity.Service.WithClock`). The
scenarios needed the missing `changePlan`/`getSubscription`/`cancelSubscription`
surface — implemented here as the US-11.3 CORE (recorded, not silent):
upgrades immediate; CLEAN downgrades pend to the anchor (`pending_plan`/
`pending_plan_at`, applied by the lifecycle sweep); blocked downgrades 409 with
EVERY reason verbatim + remediation (B4). `dunning{}`/`wind_down{}` are derived
projections of status + timestamps.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] QA scenario 3 runs in seconds: fail card → day-8 provisioning_paused with
  `day:8` + retries listed → payment clears everything instantly → suspended at
  day 21+ keeps state → day-89 assert NOTHING was deleted (90-day rule never
  earlier).
- [x] QA scenario 4 runs in seconds: Business→Pro with 12 members + 4 projects
  → 409 carrying BOTH reasons verbatim ("12 members > Pro's 5 included seats",
  "4 projects > Pro's 3 project limit"); clean downgrade pends and applies at
  the warped anchor with `orgs.plan` converged.

## Out of scope

- Stripe-driven retries (T11.4); proration (ledger); the canon's "2 cells"
  downgrade reason (cells aren't data until E1 runtime — finding recorded).

## Related

QA #3/#4 · T11.2 (state machine) · US-11.3 (remainder: proration) · B4/B12.

## Outcome

The Q9 harness is a shared `warpedClock` driven through `WithClock` seams —
both scenarios complete in ~4s of wall clock while traversing 100+ simulated
days. Landing it surfaced and fixed a REAL cross-source bug: `commit` updated
`subscriptions.plan` but never `orgs.plan` (what every plan gate reads), so a
cancel-at-anchor left an org gated as paid forever — `SyncOrgPlan` now converges
them on every state write, and scenario 4 asserts the convergence after the
anchor apply. The subscription HTTP surface (get/changePlan/cancel) landed as
the US-11.3 core: the B4 all-reasons-verbatim 409 via a `problem.Carrier`
error, pending-downgrade-at-anchor via `pending_plan(_at)` swept by
`AdvanceLifecycle`, upgrades immediate (proration = tracked ledger item).
Findings recorded: the canon's "cells" downgrade reason is unevaluable until
cells are data (E1); dunning payment retries are schedule-only until T11.4.
