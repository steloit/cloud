## Goal

RBAC evaluator with rbac-matrix.csv loaded as data

## Summary

Y = ceiling (policies may narrow), N = final (nothing widens).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] matrix is data, not code; table-driven test over every cell.

## Outcome

Delivered 2026-07-19. The matrix is DATA end-to-end: rbac-matrix.csv stays owned in
docs/product/11-permissions/, is copied into the module by `make gen-go` (drift fails CI),
embedded, and parsed strictly at boot (unknown cells/columns/dupes fail fast). Two-layer
evaluator per ADR-0006: matrix N is final, Y consults the PolicyLayer seam (T2.4's typed-Go
narrowing plugs in; policies can deny, never grant — test-proven both directions). Every
denial explains itself (role:<r> lacks <p> · membership:none · policy:<name>) feeding the
E3 403 grammar. The matrix's own special cell — ai.apply_proposal = "per underlying
action" — is modeled first-class as a DELEGATED permission (AI Law 1 as data): direct
checks deny with the re-evaluate instruction. orgs+members tables land (timestamped
migration; T2.2's deferred org-key FK attached); Authorizer binds evaluator to membership.
Tests: every-cell sweep through the public Check path, narrows-never-widens, delegated
denial, unknown-permission deny-by-default, membership integration (owner/dev/billing/
outsider, duplicate-membership rejection) against real Postgres in CI.
